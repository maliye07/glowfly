Frequently asked questions

How do I open the parent area?

Tap Parent area on the home screen. The first time, you set a 4-digit PIN that your child doesn't know.

I forgot my PIN.

On the PIN screen tap Forgot PIN and answer the adult check question. You can then set a new PIN. Game data is not deleted.

I have more than one child.

Create a separate profile for each child in the parent area (up to 5 with the Family Plan). Each profile has its own difficulty and progress.

How do I cancel the Family Plan?

On iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Glowfly, or in the app tap Parent area → Family Plan → Manage subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period.

Can we use the Family Plan on my partner's phone too?

Yes. The Family Plan supports Family Sharing: up to 6 people in your Apple family group can use it without paying again. On the other device tap Parent area → Family Plan → Restore purchases. Game history is kept separately on each device.

I switched phones and the Family Plan is gone.

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Parent area → Family Plan → Restore purchases. Game history is stored on the device and doesn't move to the new phone; use the "progress file" to send progress to a specialist.

I'd like a refund.

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases: reportaproblem.apple.com

There's no sound.

Make sure the silent switch on the side of the iPhone is off, and that Sound and Music are on in the app's settings.

Where is our data stored?

A child's game data is stored only on your device and never sent to us. Details: Privacy Policy.

Does Glowfly treat ADHD?

No. Glowfly is a focus game that exercises attention, impulse control and working memory; it is not a diagnostic or treatment tool. Talk to a health professional about your child.

Specialists and institutions

Specialists can view the progress files families send in the specialist panel and create home assignment codes. Write to us about institution licenses.

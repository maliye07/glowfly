Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

In short: Glowfly has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. A child's game data stays on their own device and is never sent to us. When you make a purchase, Apple processes the payment and the purchase is verified through RevenueCat using an anonymous ID.

1. Who we are

This policy is provided under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and applicable children's privacy laws such as COPPA. Data controller: Akif Fakıoğlu ("Glowfly", "we"). Contact: info@akif.com.tr

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on the device where the app is installed. We have no servers; it never reaches us.

InformationPurpose
Child profile: a name or nickname typed by the parent, a chosen colourKeeping siblings' progress separate
Game sessions: date, time, focus score, attention, impulse control, working memory and stopping-margin measuresAdapting difficulty to the child and showing progress in the parent area
Settings: sound, music, haptics, calm mode, symbol mode, language, selected world, daily session limitRunning the app the way you prefer
Parent PIN (stored only as an irreversible hash)Protecting the parent area and purchase screen from the child
Home assignment from a specialist (weekly goal, note)Showing the assignment in the app

You don't need to enter the child's real name; a nickname is enough.

3. Purchases

Family Plan payments are processed by Apple (App Store). We never see your card or payment details. To verify purchases and let you restore them on other devices we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), which receives:

  • A random anonymous user ID created by the app (not your name, email or Apple ID)
  • The product purchased, subscription status and history, country and currency
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system and app version, and IP address

Child profiles and game results are not sent to RevenueCat. This processing is necessary to perform the purchase contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and involves a transfer of data outside Turkey and the EU. Details: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

PDF reports, text summaries and the "progress file" are shared only when you tap share, using your device's share sheet, with the person you choose (for example your child's specialist). We never see them.

5. Specialist panel

The web panel for specialists is serverless too. Progress files a specialist adds stay in that specialist's browser and are not sent to us.

6. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking and no behavioural analytics. It works offline; the internet is used only for purchases.

7. Children's privacy

Glowfly is designed for parents to use with their children. We do not collect personal information from children. The purchase screen, settings and links that leave the app are inside the PIN-protected parent area.

8. Retention and deletion

You can delete on-device information at any time: deleting a child profile in the parent area deletes all of its sessions, and uninstalling the app deletes all data. Purchase records at RevenueCat are kept for the period described in RevenueCat's policy for legal reasons and to allow restoring purchases; you can ask for deletion at info@akif.com.tr.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Contact us at info@akif.com.tr; we reply within 30 days.

10. Changes

When we update this policy we publish the new version on this page with its effective date and announce significant changes in the app.
